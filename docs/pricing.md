# Pricing page

The public `/pricing` route lists three proposed plans in Nigerian naira.
The user approved these draft prices and allowances on November 24, 2026.

| Plan | Price | Voice allowance | Saved decks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Rehearsal | Free | 30 minutes once | 3 |
| Spotlight | ₦20,000 per month | 300 minutes per month | 20 |
| Headline | ₦50,000 per month | 1,000 minutes per month | 60 |

All plans list the current presentation tools. This change adds public pricing
content only. It does not add payments, subscription state, or usage enforcement.
The page states this limit. Paid actions are disabled and labelled coming soon.
The free action opens `/login`. No plan entitlement is granted by a link.
The page reuses the existing brand fonts, colors, header, and footer.

The route remains available by direct URL. The header and footer do not link to it.
